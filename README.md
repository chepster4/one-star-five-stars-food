Entry for the [One Star, Five Stars for the Food](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7) bounty on make.fun — one sentence, at most 200 characters: a one-star review of Earth written by an alien tourist that still compliments the food.

## The entry

> One star: Earth charged me for water, made me queue behind humans apologizing to vending machines, and rained on my antenna, but the charcoal satay at midnight almost made me extend my stay.

190 characters, one sentence.

## Why this sentence

Three petty, distinctly alien grievances — paying for water, queueing behind humans who apologize to vending machines, rain on an antenna — then the turn: the charcoal satay at midnight is so good the tourist almost extends its stay. The food compliment, not the complaint, wins the sentence. No generic complaint list, no borrowed competitor lines.

## How to run

Requires Node 22 or later (no dependencies, standard library only).

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` writes the sentence above to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline. Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
